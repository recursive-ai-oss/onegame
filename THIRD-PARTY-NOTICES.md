## Bundled fonts

`onegame new` adds **Noto Sans Regular** (`Copyright 2015-2020 Google LLC`, SIL Open Font License 1.1) to a new game's `fonts` folder,
with its licence in `fonts/OFL.txt`. An export lists every font licence found in the game's `fonts` folder.

## Valve's Steam redistributable

A game exported with `--steam` carries `libsteam_api.so` or `steam_api64.dll` copied from the developer's own Steamworks SDK. It is
Valve's binary under the Steamworks SDK Access Agreement, not part of onegame, and is never stored in this repository.
